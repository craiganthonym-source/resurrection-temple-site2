Our Story

One Empire.
One Vision. One Standard.

Resurrection Temple Ministries is the spiritual foundation of the RT Dynasty — a faith-driven movement built on purpose, discipline, and the relentless pursuit of Kingdom legacy. Founded by Craig A. Mayweather in West Covina, CA.

The Foundation

Built on Faith.
Driven by Purpose.

Resurrection Temple Ministries was established as the spiritual cornerstone of a broader Kingdom vision — a ministry that doesn't just preach but builds, equips, and restores. Every division of the RT Dynasty operates from this foundation.

Our approach is straightforward: truth delivered with conviction, community served with compassion, and wealth built with Kingdom principles at the center.

We believe that spiritual restoration and practical empowerment are inseparable. Faith without works means nothing. Works without faith means the same.
