About RT Investments

Private Capital. Purposeful Strategy.

RT Investments is the capital arm of the Resurrection Temple Dynasty — a vertically integrated, multi-entity brand empire. We don't operate as a retail investment firm. We work selectively with aligned individuals, family offices, and private operators who understand the value of disciplined, relationship-first capital deployment.

Our Focus

We concentrate on real estate, private lending, and business equity — sectors where relationships and expertise create compounding advantage.

Our Standard

Every engagement is governed by the principles of Ma'at — truth, balance, order, and reciprocity. We don't do volume. We do alignment.

Our Structure

Operating beneath RT Holdings (a Delaware Statutory Business Trust), RT Investments maintains institutional-grade governance with the speed and discretion of a private firm.

What We Do

Capital Services

From private consultation to active capital deployment, RT Investments offers a structured range of services for qualifying clients.

Real Estate Capital Partnerships

We partner with developers, operators, and investors on residential, commercial, and mixed-use real estate opportunities across Greater Los Angeles and beyond.

Short-term bridge loans and private notes for qualified borrowers. Asset-backed. Relationship-driven. Structured for speed and certainty of close.

Capital Partner Presentations

Private Consultation

How We Operate

The RT Investments Approach

Our process is deliberate and relational. We don't transact — we build partnerships designed to generate compounding returns over time.

01 — Inquiry & Intake

Submit your inquiry through our secure portal. Provide context on your objectives, capital range, and timeline. Every submission is reviewed within 24–48 hours.

02 — Private Briefing

Qualified inquiries proceed to a private briefing call with a RT Investments representative. No pressure. Just clarity and alignment discovery.

03 — Structured Proposal

If there's alignment, we deliver a structured proposal outlining terms, returns, timeline, and risk profile — clearly documented and reviewed together.

04 — Deployment & Management

Upon execution, capital is deployed under institutional-grade documentation, with regular reporting, transparency, and direct principal access.
