The Curriculum

9 Complete Modules

Each module builds on the last — creating a complete system for wealth creation, protection, and legacy transfer.

Module 1

Entity Formation & LLC Setup

How to structure your business entities for maximum protection, tax advantage, and scalability. LLC stacking, registered agents, operating agreements, and multi-entity architecture.

Module 2

Business Credit Strategy

Building a fundable business from scratch. EIN, DUNS, Paydex scores, vendor tradelines, and scaling from starter credit to six-figure business lines — step by step.

Module 3

Tax Planning & Strategy

Legal tax reduction strategies used by business owners and investors. Entity election, deductions, depreciation, cost segregation, and working with a tax strategist vs. a tax preparer.

Module 4

Real Estate Fundamentals

Acquiring, financing, and holding real estate inside entities. BRRRR, subject-to, creative financing, and using real estate as the foundation of a wealth-building portfolio.

Module 5

Infinite Banking Concept

How to become your own bank using whole life insurance. Policy design, paid-up additions, policy loans, and using IBC to finance your own investments and business operations.

Module 6

Life Insurance & IUL

Index Universal Life as a wealth-building vehicle. Cash value accumulation, index crediting strategies, death benefit planning, and the role of IUL in a complete financial plan.

Module 7

Legacy & ILIT Planning

Irrevocable Life Insurance Trusts, dynasty trusts, and wealth transfer strategies. How to structure your estate so your family receives maximum benefit with minimum tax exposure.

Module 8

Asset Protection

Shielding your wealth from lawsuits, creditors, and predators. Charging order protection, series LLCs, Delaware Statutory Business Trusts, and multi-layer entity defense.

Module 9

Dynasty Blueprint

Assembling all nine modules into a complete, integrated wealth system. Your personal dynasty blueprint — entity stack, insurance stack, real estate stack, and legacy architecture in one unified plan.
