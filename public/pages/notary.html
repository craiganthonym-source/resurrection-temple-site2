A mobile notary travels to your location — your home, office, hospital, or any convenient location — rather than requiring you to come to them. RT Notary Solutions serves all of Southern California, making it easy to get documents notarized without leaving your location.
You must bring a current, government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID). All signers must be present with valid ID. Bring your unsigned documents — do not sign them in advance. The notary must witness your signature in person.
We offer same-day appointments in most areas of Southern California, depending on availability. For urgent situations such as hospital visits or real estate closings, call us directly at (626) 479-1082 and we will do our best to accommodate you immediately. Evening and weekend slots are available.
Yes. Loan signings are one of our most requested services. Our notaries are certified signing agents trained to handle full loan packages including refinance closings, purchase closings, HELOC documents, and more. We work regularly with title companies, escrow offices, and lenders throughout SoCal.
Absolutely. We provide bedside notary services at hospitals, rehab centers, nursing homes, and assisted living facilities throughout Southern California. An apostille is a certification that authenticates a document for use in foreign countries that are part of the Hague Convention. We first notarize your document, then coordinate the apostille process through the California Secretary of State. Common documents include birth certificates, marriage certificates, diplomas, and business documents for international use.
Yes. We provide Live Scan fingerprinting services for employment background checks, professional licensing, adoption applications, and other purposes. Call ahead to schedule and confirm availability in your area.
A notary's role is to verify identity and witness signatures — not to draft legal documents. However, RT Ministries Consultants division offers document drafting services including LLC formation, operating agreements, trust formation, contracts, and more. Ask us about a referral for document preparation services.
We accept cash, Zelle, and major credit/debit cards. Payment is due at the time of service. For large loan signing packages or scheduled appointments, arrangements can be made in advance.
