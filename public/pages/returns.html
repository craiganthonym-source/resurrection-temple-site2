Return Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

All Sales Are Final

All purchases made through RT Shop are final and non-refundable. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or issue refunds for any product sold through this platform, including but not limited to:

  • Books and printed materials
  • Digital downloads and workbooks
  • Music and audio products (CDs)
  • Apparel (hoodies, polo shirts, and any clothing items)
  • Accessories (keychains and other physical goods)
  • Vendor-listed consignment products

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive an item that is damaged or defective upon arrival, you must notify RT Shop within 48 hours of delivery by emailing cmayweather@ResurrectionTemple.org with your order details and photos of the damaged item. We will review each case individually and, at our sole discretion, may offer a replacement of the same item if inventory is available.

Order Errors

If you received the wrong item due to an error on our part, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will correct the error at no cost to you.

Digital Products

Due to the nature of digital goods, all digital product sales are final immediately upon delivery of the download link or file. No exceptions will be made.

Apparel Sizing

Please review sizing information carefully before purchasing apparel. RT Shop does not accept returns or exchanges due to sizing issues. Size charts are available upon request by emailing us before placing your order.
