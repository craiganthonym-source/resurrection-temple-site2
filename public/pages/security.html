About RT Security

Professional Security. Principled Command.

RT Security is the protective services division of the Resurrection Temple Dynasty. We field trained, licensed security professionals who operate with the highest levels of discipline, integrity, and professionalism. Whether you need event security, executive protection, or ongoing site coverage, RT Security brings command presence backed by the RT standard of excellence.

Our Officers

All RT Security officers are licensed, background-checked, and trained to de-escalate, protect, and respond with calm authority in any environment.

Our Standard

We operate by the principles of Ma'at — justice, order, and truth. Our officers are not just guards; they are ambassadors of protection and professionalism.

Our Coverage

Serving the San Gabriel Valley, Greater Los Angeles, and Inland Empire. Available for single events, short-term contracts, and ongoing security programs.

What We Provide

Security Services

From one-day events to long-term contracts, RT Security delivers trained personnel and professional oversight across every engagement type.

Event Security

Uniformed and plain-clothes officers for corporate events, concerts, galas, community gatherings, and private celebrations. Crowd management, access control, and incident response included.

Executive & VIP Protection

Discreet close-protection officers for executives, dignitaries, pastors, and high-profile individuals. Route planning, advance work, and 24-hour coverage available.

On-Site Security Officers

Stationary and roving guards for commercial properties, construction sites, warehouses, retail establishments, and residential communities. Armed and unarmed options available.

Mobile Patrol

Regular patrol rounds for properties, parking facilities, multi-tenant buildings, and campuses. Visible deterrence with documented check-in reports after every pass.

Church & Ministry Security

Specialized security programs for houses of worship — protecting congregations during services, special events, and large gatherings. Sensitivity-trained, faith-aware officers.

Loss Prevention

Retail loss prevention officers trained in observation, deterrence, and lawful apprehension procedures. Reduce shrinkage and protect your bottom line.
