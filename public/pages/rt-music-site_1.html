The Artist

From the Streets of
Los Angeles
to the Kingdom

K.E.R.M. is Craig Anthony Mayweather — founder and president of Resurrection Temple, a multi-entity faith organization headquartered in West Covina, CA. The music came first: a young man from the City of Angels, shaped by the streets, redirected by God.

The album By Design is not a performance — it's a testimony. Blending soul, R&B, and worship into Gospel Fusion, every song is a chapter of a real redemption story that now anchors a 17-entity ministry empire built to serve, educate, and liberate.

RT Music is not just a label. It is the sound of the Dynasty.
