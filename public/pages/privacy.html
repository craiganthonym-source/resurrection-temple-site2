Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2025

1. Who We Are

Resurrection Temple (“RT,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a multi-entity brand enterprise headquartered in San Dimas, California, operating across seventeen divisions including construction, transportation, assisted living, investments, notary services, consulting, automotive, apparel, ministry, and education. Our primary website is www.resurrectiontemple.com. Questions about this policy may be directed to cmayweather@ResurrectionTemple.org.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a contact, inquiry, or intake form on our website
  • Request a quote, consultation, or service from any RT division
  • Purchase a product such as the Dynasty Wealth System™
  • Subscribe to communications or updates
  • Communicate with us by email or phone

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, business name, and any details you include in your messages or intake forms.

We also collect standard technical data automatically when you visit our site, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs, through standard web server logs and analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and service requests
  • Deliver services you have requested or contracted for
  • Process transactions and send related information
  • Route your inquiry to the appropriate RT division
  • Send operational communications such as confirmations and updates
  • Improve our website, services, and client experience
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • RT division staff and management — to fulfill your service request
  • Service providers — vendors who assist with operations (email delivery, payment processing, analytics) under confidentiality obligations
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain business records, and comply with our legal obligations. Contact and inquiry records are generally retained for a minimum of three years.

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party platforms including Gumroad, Etsy, and other external services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those platforms. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at cmayweather@ResurrectionTemple.org. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
